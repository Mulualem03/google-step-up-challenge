Why Display is out

It looks cheap on impressions but fails everywhere it counts.

  • 1
    Cheapest CPM, worst CPA.
    $4 CPM but $57 CPA, 10× more expensive per sign-up than Search.
  • 2
    Zero significant brand lift.
    All 16 Display brand-lift studies were non-significant. Whatever lift exists is statistical noise.
  • 3
    Brief is 8 weeks, not always-on.
    Awareness-only formats need long flighting to compound. We don't have that time.
